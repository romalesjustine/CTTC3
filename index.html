About NutriPro

NutriPro is a Tier-3 Advanced application designed to help users address their nutritional needs through a smart, intuitive dashboard interface.

Data Source

All nutritional data within this application is derived from the official U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) MyPyramid Food Raw Data. The raw data has been transformed to ensure lightning-fast searching capabilities.

How the Math Works

The "Target Setup" calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to determine your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) based on the weight, height, and age you provide. It then applies a standard activity multiplier to generate your Daily Goal.

Privacy

The AI Mood Recommender runs 100% locally in your browser using TensorFlow.js. No camera data or images are ever recorded, saved, or sent to a server.
